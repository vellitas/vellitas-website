Privacy

Effective September 17, 2026

Overview

This notice explains how Vellitas, LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Vellitas," "we," or "us"), handles information associated with this public website and communications sent to us.

Information associated with a visit

The website does not provide user accounts and does not currently use advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics. Like most web servers, our server may record technical request information such as an IP address, timestamp, requested URL, referring page, browser identifier, and response status.

We use server records to operate the website, diagnose errors, understand aggregate traffic, and investigate misuse or security events. We retain them only as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Communications

If you use the briefing form, we receive the name, work email, organization, optional role, and message you submit. We use that information to respond, evaluate a potential business relationship, prevent abuse, and maintain appropriate business records. The form stores an HMAC-derived value rather than the raw source IP address.

Retention

Contact requests are normally retained for up to 24 months after the last substantive interaction. Routine website and security logs are normally retained for 30 days. Details about assessment data, historical observations, backups, and exceptions appear in our data and responsible-scanning practices.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may use infrastructure, email, security, and professional service providers to operate our business. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a lawful corporate transaction.

External links

The website links to patent records and professional profiles operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.

Your choices

You may ask about information you have provided to us or request that we correct or delete it, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be submitted through the Vellitas contact form.
